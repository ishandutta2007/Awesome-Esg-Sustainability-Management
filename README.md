# Awesome-Esg-Sustainability-Management

## Top ESG & Sustainability Management Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Emissions Accounting, CSRD Reporting & Decarbonization Planning*

**Last updated: March 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **ESG & Sustainability Management**. These tools help organizations measure Scope 1, 2, and 3 emissions, prepare CSRD-aligned sustainability reports, manage ESG data collection, and track decarbonization progress.



**Examples** include Salesforce Sustainability Cloud, Persefoni, Watershed, Sweep, Plan A, Sinai Technologies, Greenly, Sphera, Emitwise, and Normative (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for self-hosting, transparent emissions calculation, and open ESG reporting — ideal for sustainability teams, compliance officers, and developers seeking auditable, vendor-independent carbon accounting.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[Salesforce Sustainability Cloud](https://www.salesforce.com/products/net-zero-cloud/)**

  Enterprise carbon accounting platform built on Salesforce. Features Scope 1/2/3 emissions tracking, supplier engagement, and CSRD-ready reporting with audit trails.



- **[Persefoni](https://persefoni.com/)**

  Climate management and accounting platform (CMAP) for enterprises and financial institutions. Features PCAF-aligned financed emissions, CSRD compliance, and audit-ready reporting.



- **[Watershed](https://watershed.com/)**

  Enterprise climate platform for carbon accounting, disclosure, and decarbonization. Features supplier data collection, CSRD/ISSB reporting, and clean energy procurement.



- **[Sweep](https://www.sweep.net/)**

  Carbon and ESG data management platform. Features Scope 1/2/3 tracking, supplier engagement, CSRD reporting, and decarbonization planning.



- **[Plan A](https://plana.earth/)**

  ESG and carbon accounting software for businesses. Features automated emissions calculation, CSRD/ESRS reporting, and net-zero target setting.



- **[Sinai Technologies](https://www.sinai.com/)**

  AI-powered Carbon Management & ESG Compliance platform for enterprise complexity. Calculation methodology certified by TÜV Rheinland against GHG Protocol and ISO 14064. Features Scope 1–3 automation, supplier engagement, financial impact modeling, and audit-grade data. Partnership with GIST Impact adds climate risk analytics and double materiality assessment .



- **[Greenly](https://greenly.earth/)**

  Carbon accounting platform for SMEs and mid-market. Features automated data collection, Scope 1/2/3 calculation, and CSRD-ready reporting.



- **[Sphera](https://sphera.com/)**

  ESG performance and risk management platform. Features corporate and product carbon footprints, supply chain sustainability, and regulatory reporting.



- **[Emitwise](https://emitwise.com/)**

  Carbon accounting and reporting platform for complex supply chains. Features Scope 3 mapping, CSRD compliance, and supplier engagement.



- **[Normative](https://normative.io/)**

  Carbon accounting engine for enterprises. Features science-based targets, CSRD reporting, and full supply chain emissions tracking.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[OpenGHG Carbon Calculator](https://github.com/mindsongreen/OpenGHG)**

  Open-source, transparent carbon footprint calculator (license varies) designed for robust, auditable, and methodology-driven carbon inventories . Key principles include separation of data domains, federated database architecture allowing customer-hosted sensitive data, explicit unit algebra, and full traceability from input to results. Backend PHP/PostgreSQL.



- **[CarbonInk](https://github.com/lxzxl/carbonink)**

  Free, open-source, local-first GHG carbon accounting for desktop (MIT). Features AI extraction from utility bills and receipts, ISO 14064-1 inventory reports, Scope 1/2/3 tracking with audit-grade calculation snapshots, and built-in MCP server. Runs fully offline — data never leaves the device. macOS + Windows .



- **[carbon-footprint-mcp](https://pypi.org/project/carbon-footprint-mcp/)**

  MCP server for calculating organizational carbon footprints from bank statements and financial exports using EPA GHG emission factors (MIT). Runs 100% locally, sends no financial data to external APIs. Computes Scope 1/2/3 emissions, scores carbon intensity by revenue/headcount, and generates HTML/Markdown reports .



- **[ESG-Report-Studio](https://github.com/SebastianDabkowski/ESG-Report-Studio)**

  ESG responsibility report platform (license TBD) supporting structured, controlled, and auditable ESG reporting . Treats ESG report as management and compliance artifact. Features reporting scope definition, report structure configuration, data collection/validation, ownership assignment, completeness tracking, assumptions documentation, and CSRD/ESRS mapping. Includes audit data retention policies and role-based access controls.



- **[ESG-Copilot](https://github.com/omkardongre/ESG-Copilot)**

  AI-powered ESG compliance automation platform (MIT) with enterprise-grade security. Features 5 AI agents: Regulation Research, ESG Data Collection, Emissions Calculator (Scope 1/2/3 via Climatiq API), Report Generator (GRI/SASB/TCFD), and Chat Agent (permission-aware RAG).



- **[ESG Reporting AI](https://github.com/shashank-dj/esg-reporting-ai)**

  AI-augmented ESG reporting platform combining deterministic ESG calculations with grounded AI copilots for audit-ready, CSRD-aligned reporting . Features Scope 1/2 calculation, Scope 3 spend-based estimation, audit readiness scoring (0-100), CSRD/ESRS E1 mapping, GRI mapping, and AI Narrative Copilot with deterministic fallback.



- **[GreenOps](https://github.com/cherryaugusta/greenops-carbon-accounting-platform)**

  Full-stack ESG carbon accounting platform (license varies) with Django, DRF, React, and Docker . Features employee carbon logging (travel and energy), automatic CO₂e calculation using emission factors, manager approval workflow, full audit trail, JWT-secured API, dashboard with charts, and drag-and-drop report builder.



- **[SDMP (Sustainability Data Management Platform)](https://github.com/panu-123/AI-powered-Sustainability-Data-Management-Platform-for-industries)**

  Full-stack Sustainability Data Management Platform using Django REST Framework and React . Features carbon emission monitoring, energy/fuel tracking, air quality monitoring, AI-powered sustainability advisor, workflow management, and PDF/CSV reporting.



- **[WILMA](https://trikon-online.w-hs.de/2025/12/wilma-assistiert-bei-der-nachhaltigkeitsberichterstattung/)**

  AI-supported assistance system for double materiality analysis (open-source from 2027). Developed at Westfälische Hochschule for SMEs. Features structured preparation phase, guided analysis process, sustainability management modules, and automated documentation. Free open-source solution from 2027 .



- **[Activity Browser](https://github.com/LCA-ActivityBrowser/activity-browser)**

  Open-source GUI for Brightway2 LCA software. Features fast LCA calculations, parameter/scenario modeling, uncertainty analysis, Sankey Diagrams, Monte Carlo, and Global Sensitivity Analysis.



- **[Lcpy](https://github.com/spirdgk/lcpy)**

  Open-source Python package for parametric and dynamic Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) and Life Cycle Costing (LCC) analysis. Provides computational environment for developing parametric and dynamic LCA-LCC models integrated with process simulations.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **[Hashgraph Guardian](https://github.com/hashgraph/guardian)** — Open-source platform for digital environmental assets and carbon markets (Apache-2.0), using Web3 for transparent verification .

- **[sustainability-ai-platform](https://github.com/ManviPanjwani/sustainability-ai-platform)** — Real-time, AI-powered sustainability platform (FastAPI + Next.js) to measure, forecast, and reduce carbon footprint .

- **[Community Sustainability Tracker](https://github.com/ssudheer1/EcoBeacon)** — Flask web app for tracking energy, water, waste, and transport emissions with gamified challenges and AI-driven recommendations .

- Community **n8n** workflows for ESG data collection automation and supplier engagement.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **CarbonInk** or **OpenGHG Carbon Calculator** as the core emissions calculation engine with **ESG-Report-Studio** for CSRD-aligned reporting structure, **ESG Reporting AI** for audit readiness scoring and AI narratives, **Lcpy** for product-level LCA analysis, and **PostgreSQL** for data persistence — creating a fully self-hosted, enterprise-grade ESG and sustainability management platform.

- Self-hosted open-source solutions require proper validation of emission factors, methodology alignment, and ongoing maintenance. Regulatory compliance, legal sign-off, and external assurance remain the responsibility of the reporting organization and its appointed auditors .
